# Candidates — Five Seconds to Explain the Glass

Judge criteria, in order (from the published judge brief):
1. One sentence ≤200 chars in which a house cat excuses knocking a glass off the table.
2. Sounds like a cat's own logic, self-interest and total lack of remorse, not a person apologising.
3. Fresh comic idea — NOT the obvious gravity / slippery-edge / it-was-already-falling lines.
4. Fits the five seconds: a spoken line a cat could plausibly deliver, not a written paragraph.
5. Uses every character well: no padding, throat-clearing or repeated joke.

Scoring: 0–5 per criterion, manual scoring (no live-judge A/B: the shared OpenRouter
key is exhausted, HTTP 402 — scores below are honest manual scores, not judge numbers).
Comparison is criteria-ordered: first criterion where two lines differ decides, unless a
later criterion differs by much more.

Live-field tropes avoided (from live-titles file, 50 titles): eviction/rent, sunbeam
squatting, staring glass, witness elimination, duel at dawn, fly test, physics
paperwork / complaints to the table, petting priorities, empty glass = failed vase,
alarm clock, rival in the glass, "testing whether that glass could fly".
